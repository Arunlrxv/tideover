**idea**, the "What did you build?" field: the problem, what was built, and how NEAR fits in (20 to 2000 characters).

> A DAO shows everyone everything, so a community can't use one for hardship help: if neighbours pay your eviction notice or hospital bill from the treasury, your name and your reason stay public forever, so people don't ask. Tideover is a private hardship fund that installs on any Sputnik DAO. A member proves in the browser that a paid-up member is asking without saying which one, and their document is sealed to a small AI clerk in a secure enclave that can only tick boxes. Depending on the amount, the claim is paid automatically, by three random members, or by the council. NEAR makes it possible: the contract checks the zero-knowledge proof with NEAR's BLS12-381 host functions and verifies the clerk's signature on chain, NEAR AI reads the document in private through OutLayer, the community's Sputnik DAO keeps and pays the money, and meta-transactions let people join without holding NEAR. It runs live on NEAR testnet.
