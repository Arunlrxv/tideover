**idea** (under 2000 characters):

> A DAO shows everyone everything, so a community can't use one for hardship: a payment for a cancer bill or an eviction notice stays public forever. Tideover is a private hardship fund that installs on any Sputnik DAO with two ordinary proposals. Members pay dues into the DAO and earn cover. When someone needs help, a zero-knowledge proof shows a paid-up member is claiming without saying which one. Their document is encrypted in the browser to a NEAR AI model in a secure enclave, which answers only in closed fields, and the contract verifies that answer on chain. Small claims pay after a veto window, mid-size claims go to three random members, and large ones go to the council as normal proposals. The AI never moves money. The public ledger shows no name and no reason, and anyone can re-check every rule. NEAR makes this possible: BLS12-381 host functions verify the proof, NEAR AI provides the private reader, Sputnik DAO keeps custody, and meta-transactions let people join without holding NEAR. It also ships a reusable anonymous membership library, shown with a secret ballot for any DAO.
